# Digital Product Research: Next Launch for The Connection Code (3 October 2026)

**Market:** Nigeria and the African diaspora. **Niches:** marriage, love and dating, focused on problems most women go through.

## How to read this
Five ideas survived. Each rests on at least two independent pieces of evidence: real women asking strangers for help, plus proof that money already changes hands. **One idea scored "Build now".** Zikoko and Selar pages could not be opened from this environment, so quotes come from forum titles, Quora and news reports rather than full first-person articles.

**Do any of the existing five books qualify?** Not as the next paid-ads launch. Every one of them solves a real problem, but each is either:
- **chronic** (she can live with it for months): *He Won't Make It Official*, *Small Matter, Big Fight*;
- **about the past**: *The Money You Gave Him*;
- **narrower**: *Will He Still Want Me With A Child?*; or
- **risky to advertise**: *Your Singleness Is Not A Spiritual Problem*.

The lesson from the first launch: women *clicked* (4%+ click-through rate) but didn't *buy*. That points to a problem women recognise but can put off, combined with a ₦4,500 price from an unknown brand. The next product should solve a problem so urgent she wants help **tonight**.

## Top pick

### 1. I Found The Chats: What To Do After You Discover He's Cheating — score 93/100 (Build now)
**Niche:** Marriage | **Buyer:** married women aged 25–45 who have just found chats, photos or proof | **Price:** ₦3,000 (diaspora $7)

**The problem:** she has just seen the messages. Her family will say "all men cheat, endure it." Her friends will gossip. She doesn't know whether to confront him, stay, leave, or protect her health first.

**Evidence:**
- The same cry for help has recurred on Nairaland for about 15 years, from thread 659353 (*"Husband Cheating"*) to thread 8094593 (*"How Do I Cope With Cheating Husband?"*).
- Typical recent titles: *"I Caught My Husband Digging Our Neighbors Wife Through Our CCTV. Pls Advise Me!"* (7141883) and *"Should I Forgive My Husband For Cheating?"* (8025469).
- *"I Caught My Husband Chatting With A Lady On Facebook & WhatsApp & Confronted Him"* runs to 11+ pages.

**What she pays for it now:**
- marriage counselling at **₦5,000–₦50,000 per session** (marriageinnigeria.ng);
- private investigators;
- spiritual "remedies".

**What exists, and why it fails her:**
- **Affair-recovery reading lists are all North American** (Shirley Glass, Janis Spring, Snyder/Baucom/Gordon). None deal with in-laws, "endure it" family pressure, or Nigerian divorce realities.
- **The local advice she actually hears is documented as harmful:**
  - a clergyman telling wives they need *"ashawo vibes"* to stop husbands cheating (May 2024);
  - a pastor telling them to *"confess righteousness out of him"*;
  - a September 2026 viral line: *"a cheating husband is better than a dead one."*

**The gap:** a calm, step-by-step plan for the first 72 hours and the first 30 days after discovery. It should cover protecting her health and money first, the exact words for confronting him, how to answer family who tell her to endure, and how to decide stay or leave on her own terms.

**Contents sketch:**
1. The first 72 hours: what to do before you say a word
2. Protect yourself first: health test, money, documents
3. Confronting him: the exact words, and what his reaction tells you
4. "All men cheat": answering family, in-laws and the pastor
5. Stay or leave: a decision you make, not one made for you
6. If you stay: the conditions that make rebuilding possible
7. If you leave: the practical first steps
8. Safety and help: when it's more than cheating

**Hook:** *"You found the chats. Before you confront him, read this."*

**Channel:** Facebook marriage groups and Meta ads (women 25–45), then WhatsApp, then Selar.

**Watch out for:**
- Never counsel staying in abuse. Include helpline and legal-aid pointers.
- No "get him back" manipulation tactics.
- Keep health advice simple and neutral ("get tested").
- Meta ads must not tell the viewer what her situation is (avoid "Your husband is cheating"). Use scene hooks like *"You found the chats at 2am…"* instead.

**Why it should beat the first launch:**
1. **Urgency:** discovering cheating is a crisis. She wants help now, not "someday".
2. **Privacy:** it's the problem she can't tell anyone about, which is exactly when women buy a PDF instead of asking a person.
3. **Spending:** she already pays ₦5,000+ for a single counselling session, so ₦3,000 is easy.
4. **Married buyers** have more spending power than single women in talking stages.

## Looked at and rejected
- **Relaunching He Won't Make It Official as-is:** the problem is real but chronic, and it got clicks without sales. Fix the price and funnel before blaming the topic.
- **Small Matter, Big Fight:** low urgency.
- **The Money You Gave Him:** the pain is in the past.
- **Your Singleness Is Not A Spiritual Problem:** religion-adjacent ad risk; sell it organically.
- **Will He Still Want Me With A Child?:** narrower audience.
- **A fertility or trying-to-conceive guide:** needs medical claims, so disqualified.
- **"How to stop him cheating" or "get him back":** manipulation framing, reframed into idea #1.

## Market notes
- **Acute beats chronic for cold ads.** Pick problems where she has just found out, not ones where she's still waiting.
- **Launch at ₦2,500–₦3,000** with a diaspora price of $6–7. ₦4,500 from an unknown brand stalled.
- **Keep the funnel fixes** from the first launch: confirm tracking, offer a WhatsApp option with a free sample, show inside-page previews, and add a refund promise.
- **A natural bundle later:** I Found The Chats plus Stop Checking His Status, for the woman who leaves.
