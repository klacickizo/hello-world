# The Honest Body: Brand Kit

## Who we are
Practical health and wellness guides for African women, built on the food and drinks they already know. No foreign detox teas, no starving, no miracle promises.

**Tagline:** Feel good with the food you already know.
**Short line (logo lockup):** Real food • Honest advice
**Audience:** Women aged 22–45 in Nigeria first, then Ghana, Kenya and the diaspora. Busy social lives: owambe, weddings, church and family events.

## The honest promise (our rules)
Every book and every post must follow these, because the name promises it:
1. **No miracle claims.** Say "may" and "research suggests" when the evidence is small. Never say "cure", "burn fat fast" or "flush toxins".
2. **A safety box in every book**, covering pregnancy, medication and conditions, plus when to see a doctor instead.
3. **No before-and-after photos and no body shaming.** We talk about how you *feel*, not how your body looks.
4. **No fake testimonials.** Share real feedback only, with permission.
5. **Local food first.** Every plan uses food from a Nigerian or African market.

## Voice
Warm, plain-spoken, a little playful, like a well-read big sister. Short sentences. Local references (owambe, aso ebi, pap, swallow) used naturally.
